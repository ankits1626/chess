# Lesson 2: Connection Management - Questions

**Instructions**: Write your questions here as you work through the experiments. Don't worry about "stupid questions" - they're all valuable learning opportunities!

## Template

Use this format for your questions:

```
### Q: [Your question here]

**Context**: [What experiment were you doing? What did you observe?]

**What I think**: [Your hypothesis or what you're confused about]
```

---

## Example Questions

### Q: Why is pingPeriod = (pongWait * 9) / 10 instead of just pongWait?

**Context**: In Experiment 1, I saw pings every 54 seconds but the timeout is 60 seconds.

**What I think**: Maybe it's a safety margin? Like sending the ping early to account for network delays?

---

### Q: What happens if a client never responds to pings at all?

**Context**: In Experiment 6, I blocked pongs and the connection closed after 60 seconds.

**What I think**: The server detects it's dead. But what if the client maliciously ignores pings but keeps the TCP connection open?
